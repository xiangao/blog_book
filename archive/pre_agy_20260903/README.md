Reason for replacement: the prose was wordy, unfocused and incoherent.

Analyses that exist only in these copies, not in the shipped chapters — the reason
to keep them:

- **gwg**: RIF regression / quantile decomposition of the gap; Lee bounds under
  selection; `sensemakr` sensitivity analysis ("how strong would $U$ have to be");
  a selection-cost simulation; the two-treatments adjustment-set argument
  (`sec-two-treatments`); the closing causal-vs-constitutive section
  (`sec-constitutive`, Lily Hu / Kohler-Hausmann).
- **likert-scale-variance**: exact vs empirical derivation of the ceiling; the
  finite-sample refinement; "what belongs in the dispersion submodel"; four
  candidate models side by side.
- **equivalence-testing**: the COS Registered Reports desk-rejection criterion and
  0.9/0.95 power floor; the 131,000:1 demonstration of the Bayes-factor unit trap;
  the Cauchy prior scale ($r = 0.707$) as a hidden equivalent of $\Delta$.

The six originals also carry 41 `{#eq-...}` Quarto crossref anchors; the shipped
versions keep one.
