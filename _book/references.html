References

Each chapter lists the works it cites at the foot of its own page. This is the combined list, and it prints only works actually cited somewhere in the book, so an entry here means some chapter rests on it. The bibliography source is references.bib in the book repository.

Many entries were carried over unchanged from the bibliographies of the two causal econometrics guides, keeping the same citation keys, so a passage can move between the three books without a key being renamed.

The conjoint-analysis chapter is pinned to a frozen build, because radiant cannot be installed on the current R, and a frozen chapter does not register its citations with this list. Its two works are Hainmueller, Hopkins, and Yamamoto (2014) and Kuhfeld, Tobias, and Garratt (1994).
